Chapter 1: A Bouquet on a Grave

Katsuki had a few regrets in his life. The biggest of which he could never manage to forget. Even in the dead of night as his classmates slept, the nightmares haunted him. Echoes of blood splattered on the ground. A bright grin forever silenced, broken into pieces that stabbed into his chest. He would wake in a cold sweat, staring at the ceiling as tears crept into his eyes. There were many nights Aizawa found him in the kitchen, baking something because he had nothing else to help. Those nights the hobo didn't leave him alone, even if he never asked why. As time passed and the world outside grew more dangerous, more and more of his classmates joined him at night. Sleep was hard to come by after the USJ, and almost impossible after the Hero Killer incident. Students dead. Another injured and haunted. Too many casualties too soon in their year. Icy Hot wasn't the worst, he'd admit that much. He was also one of the few who likely understood how Katsuki felt to a degree. They both got someone killed, though one of them wasn't solely responsible. Katsuki would often find him sitting on the couch in the dorms, staring at the shrine their classmates built for those they lost. Tenya Iida, Asui Tsu. Two students who should have lived, but didn't. They weren't the only ones by the end of it. They were the last ones that the class had time to mourn though, because the months that followed only brought more death and pain. Katsuki's self-hatred grew after he was taken. He struggled with himself and figuring out where he fit and whether or not he belonged. The words the villains spouted at him in an attempt to drag him to their side echoed over and over. He really wasn't any better than them. The only thing that pushed him forward and kept him going was the knowledge that he believed he'd be a good hero. There was a note, one that Katsuki kept inside his phone case in an effort to never forget. It burned anytime he read it, pricking his fingers and choking the air from his lungs. But he kept it because he couldn't forget. He couldn't forget that Izuku took his advice and he would always regret it. He tried his hardest to be the hero Izuku saw him as. He desperately tried to do his best and save anyone he could. Even when it nearly cost him everything. Holding Eri, giving her hope- He desperately wanted to be the hero Izuku wanted. If only so the boy didn't die for nothing. The hobo was there too. He kept quiet and never pushed, and maybe that was why Katsuki broke down and told him. He told him about how disgusting he was. How lost and horrible. He told the man about Izuku, about a bright and shining star that was snuffed out because of Katsuki's insecurity. He told him about how Izuku had saved him, even when the boy chose not to save himself. How Izuku had walked away from the sludge villain with his shoulders hunched and his smile forced. Katsuki's final memory of the boy was of that stupid smile. That damned grin that never reached the nerd's eyes and always looked haunted. The last words he heard from the boy were an apology. "I'm sorry Katsuki." He hadn't registered the fact that Izuku called him by his name until it was already too late. Izuku would have been better at this. He would have worked well with Katsuki's class. He would have saved Eri without getting her hurt. He would have- But there was no way for him to do that. All Might lost his quirk because of Katsuki. Other people died at the hands of villains he couldn't stop. Even as All Might's successor stood tall, the world crumpled around them. Katsuki couldn't help but feel like it was all wrong. The blonde third-year wasn't supposed to be the one flashing a grin as he held Eri. Katsuki wasn't supposed to step in. But things kept happening and the longer that time went on the more Katsuki desperately wished that he would wake up from his nightmare. However, this wasn't just some dream. This was reality. A cruel and harsh reality he couldn't escape from. That didn't stop him from trying. As Katsuki stared up at the sky while ash fell around him, he wondered. What if Izuku was alive? Would things have gone differently if Katsuki hadn't said those stupid, horrible, bitter words? Would Izuku have let any of this happen, or would Japan as a whole be better with his smile, the real one, on their side and his ferocity next to them? Izuku would have been the best hero. He could have pushed 1-A in a way that Katsuki couldn't. He would have helped them with their quirks, mumbling like the fucking nerd he was. Those notebooks would be filled to the brim as he excitedly prattled on and on to Katsuki. He would have lost his shit talking to the heroes at UA. Aizawa would have liked him. With how much the bastard harped on them about hard work, he would have taken Izuku under his wing the second the fucking nerd opened his mouth and showed them how much of a hero he was. Katsuki would give anything, everything, to see it happen. Even if he wasn't there for it. His skin felt dry as the ash pasted against it. Japan was gone. He was here. And the world as he knew it...was just wrong. Aches echoed in his chest and he knew he would have sobbed had he the energy to do so. Tears had long since run dry and he was left with just the tired feeling pressing at his senses. He wanted to rest. What more was there to live for in this world? He'd fought so hard to make it worth it, to make a world without the nerd's stupid smile and excited rambling worth living in. But he was done. Done fighting, done trying. He closed his eyes, and as Katsuki took his final breath while kneeling in the rubble of what he once called his dream, he wondered. He wondered and dreamt of a world where those cruel words never left his lips.

Chapter 2: Spider Lilies go Well with Asphodel

His eyes opened to the sound of an alarm. He jerks, breath leaving him in sharp bursts as everything pulses and his nerves stand on end. The hair on his arms rises with goosebumps as Katsuki stares up at his ceiling. The taste of blood is still on his tongue even though he knows he's fine. He doesn't- He can't- What the fuck was that? He stares up at the ceiling as his mind whirls and everything pulses. Nothing feels right. Not the sheets under him or the blanket caging his skin. His breathing keeps picking up and his vision blurs at the edges. There's sweat caking his palms and he feels the sudden urge to grab a towel he keeps at his bedside for such things. Except he doesn't have a towel at his beside. He never has. There are thoughts in his head that don't feel like his own. Hazy workings of what should be memories but he knows are not. Can't be. In an instant he is shooting from his bed, shoving the blankets off as he stumbles and rolls over in the covers. He gasped as he hit the floor, shuddering and swallowing thickly. His limbs tremble as he ponders where those thoughts are coming from. His eyes shift, glancing at his room. The same room he has been using. And yet it feels wrong. There should be photos on his desk. There should be laughing faces as Ochako holds fingers behind his head and Kirishima grips his shoulder, the rest of the extras crowding even as he fights a smile- Something felt wrong. So very wrong. He tries to focus, but his vision is still blurry. He can't bring anything into focus. The noise around him blends together. Distantly, he feels the urge to call it a panic attack. But Katsuki doesn't have those. He never has. Even so, he can't ground himself as his panic rears its head again. He can't reconcile that everything is wrong. Which is when he hears it. Or rather when he hears her. "Oi, Katsuki, what the fuck are you doing making racket this early? Aren't you supposed to be getting ready for school brat?!" Mitsuki's voice carried through the home, up the steps leading to his room. Katsuki froze. Images rushed by again, unbidden by his struggle to meld reality with dreams. His breathing halted and then hitched before it started to pull into a semblance of rhythm His parents holding him tightly. Fire and ash raining down, too many graves- He swallows thickly and shoves the images down. He doesn't understand. A dream? A nightmare? It has to be. He knows it has to be. There's no other explanation. Not one that isn't going to make him sick. It takes him a second, but he shouts back a gruff response and pushes to his feet. His body aches as he reaches out to grab his bed and haul himself up. Then, he starts to get ready. The feeling of wrongness doesn't fade. Even as he stares at his clothes, wondering why a few shirts that he swore were torn apart in training are still there, brand new. Except he hasn't done training. Nothing beyond his quirk exercises at him and weight lifting. He's never trained like his dreams suggest. Black hair and dark eyes that watched him quietly before coming over to correct his form. Hours spent being tossed around on the matt but each time he got back up and kept swinging because that was the only thing that made him feel alive- He shakes his head and keeps going. The motions feel both familiar and foreign. He performs them exactly as he would any other day. When he finishes tugging on his gakuran, he has to pause and stare at himself in the mirror. Something just looks wrong. He doesn't like it. The neck is too tight, pressing in on his skin. Like hands, clenching against his skin and tightening. Fear courses through him because all five fingers mean he should be dead. All five fingers mean he should be ash. But Aizawa is screaming for him, and distantly, Katsuki can see red eyes. He jolts, folding over as his vision whites out again. He clenches the surface of his sink, trying to get ahold of himself. A hand jerks up and he tugs his tie loose. Only then does the panic ease. It still takes him a second as he clutches the fabric in his hand. Wrinkles form along the fabric. Something horrid swirls in his gut. He closes his eyes and has to take another moment. And even then the only thought rushing through his mind is a simple one. What the fuck? School, as it turns out, also has that somewhat foreign feeling as he sits in his seat. It feels wrong, but he also knows that he was here yesterday and everything is how it should be. It's exactly as it was the day before. He can't wrap his head around today or why he doesn't feel right. There are jeers from other members of the class, but he ignores them in favor of leaning back in his seat and kicking his feet up on the desk. He almost expects Tenya to yell at him- But Tenya isn't here. He doesn't know a Tenya. He must be going insane. Katsuki ignores the rest of the extras around him, waiting for the bell. They are all the same. He doesn't remember the last time he saw them. He only saw them a day ago. It's almost the weekend and he's ready to rest and get rid of this weird nightmare that keeps plaguing him. He doesn't care about Which is of course when a head of green hair darts inside, head ducked and arms caging a notebook to their chest. Katuski's beginning to think that he's having breathing problems because he almost feels like he got sucker punched in the gut upon seeing the boy. Izuku. Izuku who shouldn't be there. Izuku who was d- He ignores the nerd, turning to the front even as one of his classmates sticks his foot out. Izuku trips, falling forward. Before Katsuki can stop himself, he reaches out, grabs Izuku's arm, and pushes him back to his feet. Silence fills the class as Izuku jerks his head over to Katsuki with wide eyes. Why did he do that? Katsuki hides his shock behind a sneer, turning back to the front after shoving Izuku to his seat. "Sit the fuck down already, damned nerd." He ignores the prickling sensation of everyone staring at him, facing the teacher as they enter with the intent to just forget all the strange things of the day. But the incident doesn't leave his mind. No matter how long he glares at his paper and tries to figure it out. No matter how many images flit past his mind. Even at lunch, Katsuki finds himself stalling as the extras whisper loudly about hurting Izuku or shoving him around. He doesn't know why, but a sick feeling of dread fills him the more he hears. He finally decides he's had enough and grabs one of the more vocal extras, shoving him into the wall as the rest of the class leaves for lunch. Leaving Izuku and the rest of the group alone in the room. "Stop wasting your time. Be useful for once you damned extra." He snaps. His eyes burn darkly as his glare narrows on them. He doesn't know why he's so angry. Shouto ate alone. Katsuki wasn't sure when he joined Icy Hot, but he did. It was better than eating on his own, even if neither of them would admit to it. Nothing gets said. The extras shuffle off and away, leaving Katsuki alone with Izuku. He doesn't bother turning to the boy. He doesn't know why he's doing this. Not when he knows just how annoying Izuku is. Not when he could care less about the stupid nerd. Red shoes on a rooftop. Aunty Inko crying, screaming as she clings to the black body bag, even as someone tries to pull her away. They say nothing. Katsuki tugged his lunch out to eat it, ignoring the hesitant stares at his back directed by the other boy. The rest of the day continues, but things press at Katsuki's senses. He keeps expecting people who aren't there. His thoughts are filled with ghosts, and yet he can't place them as they slip by. He feels sick of it all by the end of the day, and it shortens his temper even more. Every word that passes the Extras' lips sends him tumbling into his spiral as he fights against the unseen force shoving images into his head. Class 1-A all together, images taken of them as they accosted UA's pool. Katsuki starts packing up as soon as the bell rings, never noticing the events that transpire when the teacher asks about their plans for the future. How could he when his head swam as soon as they opened their mouth? It's not until he's tugging his backpack over his shoulder, that the world stops being so hazy and things snap into place. Even then it's only because he hears the sound of Izuku gasping in pain behind him. He stalls. He's almost out the door. The world was so messy and hazy a second prior, but now it's so bright and vivid that it's almost too much. He can feel everything, from the brush of the overhead air on the back of his neck to the heat on his skin from the buzzing lights. Every noise is amplified almost tenfold, and yet none are louder than his heart in his ears. He turns, gazing over his shoulder and back at the nerd and the other extras. They have grabbed Izuku's notebook and are holding it over his head as he quietly asks for it back. Something twists inside of him. His feet move before he can stop himself. The room goes quiet again as Katsuki reaches out and grabs the notebook from the extra. Anger pulses through him in waves, but he doesn't know why. Is it the nerd, annoying him? Or is it the extras being shitty? Or, was it because Katsuki had no idea what the fuck was going on? He stares at the sky and wonders. Wonders how the world would be without him, and with Izuku instead. Images flash again. He can't help but silently stare at the notebook as his thoughts run rampant. The sounds are drowned in white noise and everything burns within him. His body feels wrong. His thoughts are wrong. There's supposed to be a scar along his right arm from where he fractured his wrist almost to the point of no return. There are supposed to be burn scars along his side. Instead, there's nothing there. Just like the words that should be in his mouth, but aren't. There are words on the tip of his tongue. He knows them. He knows them because he said them. Before. They burn and burn, hazing at the edges as he narrows his gaze on the notebook- And a small pop explodes from his palm. It's a panicked gesture that nearly makes him jump out of his skin. He's never lost control like that after working with Aizawa. Never had the lack of it- But who the fuck was Aizawa? A gasp, almost sounding pained echoes in front of him. He raises his head, and this time it doesn't feel like he's in control. Everything is like a movie playing past him. He doesn't know why or how or what- He just sees Izuku's wide eyes staring at the burned notebook in his hand. In another life, Katsuki would have tossed it out the window. In another life, Katsuki would have said the words that damned him to a life of regret. But Izuku speaks, and that's what drags him back. "K-Kaachan?" The boy asks. He sounds so hurt and tired. And why hadn't Katsuki ever realized it before? Why had he never seen the dimmed light in those once bright eyes? He knows the words. He can hear the echo of them in his ears, spilling from his lips without care. " You wanna be a hero so bad? I've got a time-saving idea for you. If you think you'll have a Quirk in your next life… Go take a swan dive off the roof!" Everything stills. The sounds of the world bleed back in. His eyes refocus and Katsuki feels as though everything just clicks. Years pile into his mind. Unbidden images that swirl and twine and build. Hazy thoughts that pulse and burn. He- He can't- He needs to run away. He can't stay here. He drops the notebook and turns sharply. He doesn't run, but it's a near thing as he leaves the classroom. The shouts of the extras trail after him but he can't focus or think. Not when the world is just too bright and all of his thoughts are plagued with a life outside of this one. Katsuki wants to throw up, but he can't. His body, this body, is both his and not. Nothing is right and yet everything is how it should be. A singular thought rings through everything. It cuts across the noise and the pulsing of his heart. How the fuck was he in the past?
